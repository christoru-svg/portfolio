AI & automation

AI to focus on what matters

I use AI, mostly Claude, at every step of my process: to explore faster in discovery, prototype earlier in design and make delivery more reliable. It doesn't replace design judgment: AI suggests, I review and decide. Research, strategy and trade-offs stay mine.

01

Explore

Get from research to first ideas faster.

User research synthesis

AI processes raw verbatims to surface recurring patterns. I review, requalify and build the insights: the analysis stays mine.

1 hour instead of half a day

Discovery wireframes

From the insights, I generate low-fidelity variants. I select, critique and refine: AI explores, I decide.

1 hour instead of 4

02

Prototype

Test an idea in real conditions, earlier.

Interactive prototypes

With Claude and Figma Make, I turn my mockups into clickable prototypes to test a journey with users from the first iterations, without tying up the engineering team. The prototypes in this portfolio's case studies were built this way.

Test earlier, iterate more often

03

Make it reliable

Ship screens that are checked and ready for developers.

UX and accessibility audits

A first automated pass on journeys and design system components (contrast, hierarchy, target size, ARIA labels…). I validate and prioritize; AI produces the structured report.

2 days instead of 1 week

Keyboard navigation and ARIA labels

From the Figma specs, I generate the keyboard navigation rules and ARIA attributes for each component, ready for front-end developers to use.

2 hours instead of 8
